## Research Experience

I am currently doing research on chromatic quasisymmetric functions
under [Tatsuyuki Hikita][hikita] at Kyoto University.

In summer 2025, I participated in the
[Imperial College London summer research exchange][imperial].

- **Rank varieties for modules over quantum complete intersections**

  [[pdf](/files/irop.pdf)] under mentor [Benjamin Briggs][briggs]

In summer 2024, I participated in the
[UMN Combinatorics and Algebra REU][umn-reu].

- **Equality and saturated Newton polytopes of Postnikov–Stanley polynomials**

  [[pdf][ps-pdf]] [[slides][ps-slides]]
  with [Katherine Tung][ktung] and [Yuchong Zhang][yzhang]
  under mentor [Shiyun Wang][swang] and TA Meagan Kenney

- **Newton polytopes of dual Schubert polynomials**

  [[arXiv][dual-schubert]]
  with [Katherine Tung][ktung] and [Yuchong Zhang][yzhang]

- **Postnikov–Stanley polynomials are Lorentzian**

  [[arXiv][lorentzian]]
  with [Katherine Tung][ktung] and [Yuchong Zhang][yzhang]

- **Mixed dimer model for exceptional type quiver representations
  and cluster algebras**

  [[pdf][dimer-pdf]] [[slides][dimer-slides]]
  with Casey Appleton and Sogol Cyrusian
  under mentor [Kayla Wright][kwright] and TA [Elise Catania][catania]

In summer 2023, I participated in the MIT math department's [SPUR][spur] program.

- **Flip graphs on self-complementary ideals of chain products**

  [[arXiv][flip-graphs]] [[slides](/files/spur_presentation.pdf)]
  with [Holden Mui][hmui] under mentor [Elisabeth Bullock][bullock]

In high school, I participated in the MIT [PRIMES-USA][primes] program.

- **The Stembridge equality for skew stable Grothendieck polynomials
  and skew dual stable Grothendieck polynomials**

  [[journal][stembridge]] with Fiona Abney-McPeek and Jakin Ng
  under mentor [Adela Zhang][azhang]

  *Algebraic Combinatorics, Volume 5 (2022) no. 2, pp. 187-208.*
